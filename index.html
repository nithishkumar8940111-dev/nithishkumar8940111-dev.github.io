03 // Standard Operating Discipline

Quality Assurance Methodology

A repeatable three-stage inspection workflow built to maximize Inter-Annotator Agreement (IAA).

01

Guideline-First Execution

Break down project SOPs into concrete edge-case rules—such as occlusion thresholds, span boundaries, and normalization formats—before labeling the first frame or token.

02

Pre-Label Verification

Treat model-generated pre-labels as rough drafts—inspect every polygon vertex, bounding box edge, and character offset for subtle drift rather than blindly accepting predictions.

03

Edge-Case Escalation

Flag ambiguous samples with clear, reproducible audit notes rather than guessing, protecting team-wide Cohen's Kappa / IAA scores and strengthening golden benchmark sets.
